## Design Notes

- **Why ESC-14.3-1 holds whole tests.** `TestSupportCollectionHigherOrderItem::uppercase()` calls `strtoupper`, so the fixture cannot be ported without it, and every test that constructs it waits (Sole, TakeUntilCanBeProxied, TakeWhileCanBeProxied, GroupBy, Map, MapFromArrays). A fixture missing that method would be partial.
- **SyncCallback** (planning experiment on oxlint 1.88 / tsc 7.0.2, temp dir): `filter<R>(cb: ((v, k) => NotPromise<R>) & ((v, k) => void))` — `c.filter(async () => true)` gives `TS2322` and `no-misused-promises`; `c.filter((v) => v > 1)` and `(v, k) => v > k` compile clean with contextual types. No lint rule or `.oxlintrc.json` change is needed.
- **Companions are not dual helpers.** They always return a Promise and exist because DIV-1 allows their callback to be async, so `@asyncRoot callback` is true under F-9 — the opposite of Story 12.1's `when` / `unless`. `eachAsync` / `mapAsync` belong to Story 14.5 (epics.md:3676).
