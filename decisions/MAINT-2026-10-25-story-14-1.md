## Amendment: how a Traversable iterates

#453 parked the runtime item. Its spec had every new Traversable's `[Symbol.iterator]` yield `[key, value]` pairs, as `PhpArray` does. The grader (`packages/assert`) reads every other iterable as its values. And neither the spine nor PORTING.md said which rule holds.

The Maintainer ruled **values, plus `entries()`**:

- A Traversable's `[Symbol.iterator]` yields its values, as a JS Array does. This covers `Collection`, `LazyCollection`, `ArrayIterator`, `ArrayObject`, `CachingIterator` and the PHP `WeakMap`.
- `entries()` yields `[key, value]` pairs and `keys()` its keys, as on a JS Map.
- `PhpArray`, the low-level PHP array, keeps its `[key, value]` iterator (AD-9.5).
- **Porting rule:** `foreach ($c as $v)` becomes `for (const v of c)`, and `foreach ($c as $k => $v)` becomes `for (const [k, v] of c.entries())`.
- The grader is unchanged.
- **Why values:** `for (const user of users)` gives each user, as a JavaScript developer expects. Pairs would have yielded `[0, user]` and needed a grader change.

PORTING.md's construct table and the spine's AD-26 table now say so. The item leaves epic 14's `waiting_on_maintainer`. It resumes from `retro-14-runtime-php-types-attempt-1.patch`, with the iterators changed to yield values and the Review Triage Log's other findings applied.
