# Maintainer ruling: Story 13.1, reflection in the runtime

The Maintainer gave this ruling on 2026-10-25 in the Maintainer's session. It approves the orchestrator's recommended option on each of Story 13.1's six Escalations (#433). The orchestrator's monitor agreed with all six before they went to the Maintainer.

## The clauses

| Escalation | Option | Clause | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESC-13.1-1 | 1 | DIV-3.2 | PHP reflection classes over `inject` metadata; `self` / `parent` Spec markers |
| ESC-13.1-2 | 1 | DIV-3.3 | `primitive`, `variadic(...)`, `inject({}, fn)`; an undeclared closure throws `RuntimeException` |
| ESC-13.1-3 | 1 | DIV-3.4 | `returns(<Spec>, fn)`, with `oneOf`, `allOf`, `self`, `static` |
| ESC-13.1-4 | 1 | DIV-12.15 | `@attribute(X, ...args)`, PHP's `Attribute`, `getAttributes` / `newInstance()` |
| ESC-13.1-5 | 1 | DIV-12.14 | `lazy()` / `proxy()` not ported; two `exceptions.jsonl` lines |
| ESC-13.1-6 | 1 | DIV-9.14 | `isCallable` edges: erased visibility, registry-only `'Class::method'` strings |

## The route

1. **Infrastructure first.** Action item `epic-13-item-1-runtime-reflection` builds the runtime half in `@xaravel/runtime` and `@xaravel/runtime/php`, as one Maintainer-approved Infrastructure change:
   - the reflection classes, limited to the members `Reflector`, `ReflectsClosures` and their tests call;
   - the Spec values `primitive`, `variadic`, `oneOf` and `allOf`, and the markers `self`, `parent` and `static`;
   - `returns(...)` and its reader;
   - `Attribute` and `attribute(...)`;
   - XT-runtime tests for each.

   No other runtime behaviour changes. The loop takes action items before Stories, so this lands first.
2. **Then Story 13.1.** It resumes and ports each Test ID line for line under these clauses, with the Skip Reasons the Escalations name:
   - testGetClassName and testEmptyClassName become `blocked-by:mail`;
   - testStringTypeName becomes `blocked-by:bus`;
   - testSelfClassName and testUnionTypeName become `blocked-by:database`;
   - testGetClassAttributes becomes `blocked-by:collections`.

   No assertion is removed or weakened.

Container (Epic 17) later re-exports the Spec values from the runtime, and builds on these reflection classes rather than adding its own.
